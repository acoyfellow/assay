01Idea

Assay is a lab notebook for agents. The brand looks like one: white paper, ink, one orange for the result, and figures drawn by hand on a pixel grid.

Every visual choice should be something a careful person would make on purpose. If a detail does not carry information, remove it.

08Rules

  1. Flat. No gradients, no blur, no glass, no drop shadows.
  2. One radius, 6 px. No pills, no nested rounded frames.
  3. Lines are rules, not boxes. A 1 px rule separates. A wash fills. Never both.
  4. One column of reading. 640 px measure. No bento grids, no side-by-side cards for effect.
  5. One dark block per page at most. Code only.
  6. Orange means the result. One number per figure, and pass. Never decoration.
  7. Every picture is a figure. Numbered, captioned, with n. Drawn on the 12 px grid.
  8. Plain headlines. One tone. No grey setup and black punchline.
