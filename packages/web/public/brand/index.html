01Idea

Assay runs a fleet of coding agents. The brand is a calm instrument: a light grey bench, white rounded panels, one dark card where the work happens, and orange for a result.

The pixel mark and the pixel icons are the hand-made part. Everything else is soft, quiet and even.

09Rules

  1. A grey bench, white panels. Every page sits on #ECECEB. Content sits in white panels with a 24–40 px radius and one soft shadow.
  2. One dark card per view. It holds the live thing: the fleet, the command, the code. Gradient from #19191A to #080809.
  3. Two-tone headlines. Quiet grey for the setup, ink for the point. Never more than two tones.
  4. Pills for controls. Nav, buttons, tags and result pills are fully round. Buttons are dark or light with a soft lift.
  5. Orange means the result. Pass, and the one number a figure is about. Never decoration.
  6. Pixels for drawings only. The mark and icons are drawn on the 12 px grid. Nothing else is pixelated.
  7. Readable first. Long text stays in one column. Code wraps on phones, nothing is cut off with an ellipsis.
