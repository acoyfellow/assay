07 · Rules

Do
  • Light question on the left, dark instrument on the right.
  • Split headlines: quiet setup, ink point.
  • One raised thing per group.
  • Shrink nested radii by the padding.
  • Soft orange only for pass. Hot orange only for live.
Do not
  • Add a brand colour. The palette is greys.
  • Put a shadow and a border on the same light card.
  • Use square corners.
  • Use bold. 500 is the heaviest weight.
  • Put gradients on light surfaces, except pills.
